# SOP: Weekly Status Reporting

Owner: Noor Haddad. Every active engagement sends a written status update and
holds a 30-minute status call, every week, same day and time, no exceptions —
including weeks where "nothing happened." Silence is how trust dies.

## The written update

Sent by the delivery lead at least 3 hours before the call, in the fixed
format below. It must fit on one screen.

```
Status: GREEN | YELLOW | RED
Done this week: (3 bullets max)
Next week: (3 bullets max)
Blockers: (who is blocked on whom, with a date)
Budget/scope notes: (only if something changed)
```

### Status definitions

- **GREEN** — on scope and on schedule.
- **YELLOW** — a risk needs a client decision within a week to stay on
  schedule. Every YELLOW names the decision and the decider.
- **RED** — schedule or scope is broken; a change order or replan is on the
  table. RED requires Maya to be informed before the client is.

## The call

30 minutes, hard stop. The written update is assumed read. The call covers
blockers and decisions only — demos happen at milestone demos, not status calls.

## Rules

- Never soften a YELLOW to keep the mood pleasant. The Cobble & Crane retro
  documents exactly what that costs.
- Blockers name a person and a date, not a team ("waiting on Dana to grant
  warehouse access, requested Tuesday").
- If the client skips two consecutive calls, Noor flags it to Theo as a
  relationship risk.
