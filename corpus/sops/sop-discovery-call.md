# SOP: Discovery Call

Owner: Theo Lindqvist. The discovery call is a 45-minute qualification and
scoping conversation with a prospect. Its output feeds the proposal and, if the
deal closes, the onboarding access list.

## Goals

1. Decide whether the prospect is a fit (see "What we refuse" in Engagement
   Models and Pricing).
2. Identify the one workflow or question-answering need with the highest
   pain-to-effort ratio.
3. Leave with enough detail to draft a fixed-scope proposal without a second
   call.

## Agenda (45 minutes)

- **0–5 min — frame.** Who we are in two sentences; confirm the prospect's goal
  for the call.
- **5–20 min — pain inventory.** "Walk me through the last time this process
  ran." Get concrete: who did what, in which tool, how long it took, what broke.
- **20–30 min — data reality check.** Where does the relevant data live? Who
  can grant access? Any compliance constraints? This section decides whether a
  Data Readiness Assessment must precede the build.
- **30–40 min — shape the engagement.** Map the pain to one of the five
  catalog services. State the price range out loud — sticker shock belongs in
  the call, not the proposal.
- **40–45 min — next steps.** Commit to a proposal date and name the decision
  maker who will read it.

## Disqualifiers

End the process politely if any of these appear:

- No identifiable owner of the data or process on the client side.
- The prospect wants us to "figure out what AI can do for us" with no concrete
  workflow. We sell outcomes, not exploration.
- Budget expectations below the Data Readiness Assessment price.

## After the call

Theo writes the discovery note (template in the docs repo) within 24 hours and
tags the proposed delivery lead for a feasibility sanity check before the
proposal goes out.
