# SOP: Project Kickoff

Owner: Maya Okonkwo. The kickoff call is 60 minutes, held within 7 business
days of contract signature (see Client Onboarding SOP), and only after every
item on the access list is confirmed working.

## Attendees

Required: delivery lead, client sponsor, the client-side process owner, Noor.
Optional: the second engineer, the client's IT contact.

## Agenda (60 minutes)

1. **Introductions and roles (5 min).** Name the delivery lead as the single
   technical point of contact, and the client sponsor as the decision maker.
2. **Scope readback (15 min).** The delivery lead reads the scope from the
   proposal, line by line, and the sponsor confirms each line out loud. Scope
   disputes surface here, not in week three.
3. **Out-of-scope list (10 min).** Read the explicit exclusions. Every
   engagement has them; unstated exclusions are how scope creep starts.
4. **Milestones and demo dates (10 min).** Walk the board milestones. Every
   engagement has a mid-point demo with real client data — a hard rule at
   Lanternfish since the Bluebird Dental retro.
5. **Working agreement (10 min).** Weekly status call day/time, response-time
   expectations (one business day both directions), and the payment-pause rule
   from the pricing doc, stated plainly.
6. **Risks and unknowns (10 min).** The delivery lead names the top three
   risks from the staffing huddle and what would trigger a scope-change
   conversation.

## Outputs

- Kickoff note (template) posted to the engagement folder within 24 hours,
  including the confirmed scope readback and the out-of-scope list.
- Board milestones dated.
- First weekly status call on the calendar.

## Rules

- No kickoff without confirmed access. No exceptions.
- The scope readback is verbatim from the proposal. If the sponsor wants
  changes, that is a change order before work starts, not a note in the margin.
