# Team Roles and Responsibilities

Lanternfish is six people. Titles are functional, not hierarchical — on any
given project the delivery lead outranks everyone, including the principals.

## The team

- **Maya Okonkwo — Principal, Delivery.** Owns delivery quality across all
  engagements. Final approver on every proposal and every handoff. Delivery
  lead on most Grounded Assistant Builds.
- **Theo Lindqvist — Principal, Growth.** Owns pipeline, pricing, and
  partnerships. Runs discovery calls (see Discovery Call SOP). Signs off on
  any quote above list price.
- **Priya Raman — Senior Automation Engineer.** Technical lead for Automation
  Sprints. Maintains the internal integration library and the tooling stack
  doc. On-call rotation lead for Care Plan incidents.
- **Sam Alvarez — AI Engineer.** Builds retrieval pipelines and evaluation
  harnesses for assistant projects. Owns the RAG Implementation Playbook and
  the Prompt Engineering Standards.
- **Noor Haddad — Operations Manager.** Owns onboarding/offboarding logistics,
  invoicing, scheduling, and the weekly status reporting cadence. First
  point of contact for clients between calls.
- **Jonah Petit — Associate Consultant.** Runs Data Readiness Assessments
  under Maya's review, drafts documentation, and staffs discovery research.

## Decision rights

| Decision                          | Owner            | Consulted        |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Accept/decline an engagement      | Theo             | Maya             |
| Quote above list price            | Theo             | Maya             |
| Declare a build ready for handoff | Maya             | Delivery lead    |
| Escalate an incident to "major"   | Priya            | Maya, client     |
| Publish changes to a playbook     | Playbook owner   | Both principals  |

## Staffing rules

Every engagement gets exactly one delivery lead who is the client's single
technical point of contact. Nobody leads more than two active engagements.
Jonah does not lead engagements yet; his assessments ship under Maya's review.
