# Lanternfish Consulting — Company Overview

Lanternfish Consulting is a six-person boutique consultancy that helps small and
mid-sized businesses put AI and workflow automation to work without hiring an
in-house engineering team. The firm was founded in 2022 in Port Alder by Maya
Okonkwo and Theo Lindqvist after they kept meeting the same problem in their
freelance work: businesses drowning in repetitive operations while "AI strategy"
vendors sold them decks instead of working systems.

## What we do

We take on short, outcome-scoped engagements in three service lines (see the
Service Catalog for details and pricing):

1. **Workflow automation** — replacing manual, error-prone processes with
   automated pipelines connected to the client's existing tools.
2. **AI assistants and chatbots** — internal or customer-facing assistants
   grounded in the client's own documents and data.
3. **Data readiness and advisory** — assessments that tell a client what their
   data can and cannot support before they spend on AI.

## Positioning

Our promise is "working software in weeks, not roadmaps in quarters." Every
engagement ships something the client can run without us. We deliberately stay
small: six people, no outsourcing, no white-labeling.

## Principles

- **Ship the boring thing first.** A reliable spreadsheet sync beats an
  impressive demo that breaks in week two.
- **The client owns everything.** Code, prompts, credentials, and documentation
  are handed off at project close (see the Project Handoff SOP).
- **Honest about limits.** If a client's data can't support what they want, the
  Data Readiness Assessment says so in writing before we take their money.
