# Service Catalog

Lanternfish sells five productized services. Anything outside these five is
custom work and requires principal sign-off (see Internal FAQ).

## 1. Automation Sprint (anchor offer)

A three-week fixed-scope engagement that automates one painful workflow
end-to-end. Includes discovery, build, testing with real data, documentation,
and a recorded handoff session. Most clients start here.

- Duration: 3 weeks
- Team: one delivery lead + one engineer
- Deliverable: a running automation plus a runbook the client's staff can follow

## 2. Grounded Assistant Build

An internal or customer-facing AI assistant that answers questions from the
client's own documents, with citations. Built on the pattern described in the
RAG Implementation Playbook: retrieval over the client's documents, grounded
generation, and a refusal path for questions the documents cannot answer.

- Duration: 4–6 weeks
- Prerequisite: passing score on the Data Readiness Assessment
- Deliverable: deployed assistant + evaluation report + admin guide

## 3. Support Chatbot Deployment

A customer-facing chatbot for support deflection, scoped to a client's FAQ and
help-center content. Follows the Support Chatbot Playbook, including the
escalation-to-human design that we consider non-negotiable.

- Duration: 4 weeks
- Deliverable: chatbot live on one channel + weekly quality dashboard

## 4. Data Readiness Assessment

A one-week diagnostic that inventories the client's data sources and scores
them for AI-readiness across access, quality, coverage, and governance. Ends
with a written go/no-go recommendation. Sold standalone or as the first week of
a Grounded Assistant Build.

- Duration: 1 week
- Deliverable: readiness scorecard + prioritized remediation list

## 5. Automation Care Plan (retainer)

Post-handoff monitoring and maintenance for systems we built: monthly health
checks, incident response per the Automation Incident Response SOP, and up to
two small change requests per month.

- Duration: monthly, cancel anytime
- Prerequisite: system built or audited by Lanternfish
