# Engagement Models and Pricing

All prices are fixed-fee, quoted in the proposal, and invoiced 50% at kickoff
and 50% at handoff. We do not bill hourly; hourly billing rewards slowness.

## Price list (2026)

| Service                     | Price (USD) | Duration    |
| --------------------------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| Automation Sprint           | $9,500      | 3 weeks     |
| Grounded Assistant Build    | $18,000     | 4–6 weeks   |
| Support Chatbot Deployment  | $14,000     | 4 weeks     |
| Data Readiness Assessment   | $3,500      | 1 week      |
| Automation Care Plan        | $1,200/mo   | monthly     |

## Discounts and adjustments

- The Data Readiness Assessment fee is credited in full against a Grounded
  Assistant Build signed within 60 days.
- Nonprofits receive 15% off any fixed-fee service.
- We do not discount the Care Plan; its margin funds incident response.

## What changes a quote

Scope drives price more than time. A quote goes above list when the engagement
involves more than three source systems, on-premises deployment, or compliance
review by the client's counsel. These require principal sign-off before the
proposal goes out.

## Payment terms

Net 14 from invoice date. Work pauses if the kickoff invoice is unpaid by day
21 — this is stated in the contract and repeated in the kickoff deck so it is
never a surprise (see Project Kickoff SOP).

## What we refuse

We turn down engagements where we would be the client's first-ever data
infrastructure ("we'll figure out where the data lives later"), fully custom
model training, and anything the Data Readiness Assessment scores below 40/100
unless the remediation work is part of the contract.
