# Client FAQ

Answers Noor sends prospects and new clients. Kept in sync with the Service
Catalog and the pricing doc — if they disagree, those docs win.

## How long does an engagement take?

Most start with the three-week Automation Sprint. Assistant builds run 4–6
weeks, chatbot deployments 4 weeks, and the Data Readiness Assessment is one
week. We quote a fixed duration in the proposal and report weekly against it.

## Do you bill hourly?

No. Every engagement is fixed-fee, invoiced 50% at kickoff and 50% at handoff,
net 14. Fixed fees mean our incentive is finishing well, not billing long.

## Who owns what you build?

You do — code, prompts, documentation, credentials, everything. Handoff
includes a runbook, a recorded walkthrough, and a live session where your team
operates the system while we watch. After offboarding we hold no access at all.

## What happens if the automation breaks after you leave?

If you're on the Automation Care Plan, incidents page our on-call rotation:
minor issues get next-business-day response, major ones four business hours.
Without the Care Plan, you have the runbook and the recorded walkthrough, and
you can re-engage us for a fix at standard rates.

## Can the assistant answer questions outside our documents?

By design, no. Our assistants refuse questions the source documents cannot
answer, and say so plainly, rather than guessing. Clients occasionally ask us
to loosen this; we don't. An assistant that guesses confidently is a liability,
and the refusal behavior is what makes the correct answers trustworthy.

## Do you train an AI model on our data?

No. We use retrieval — your documents are searched at question time and the
model answers from what it finds, with citations. No fine-tuning on your data,
and your data is never used to train anyone's models. Provider data-retention
terms are reviewed with you before anything is sent to any vendor.

## Why did you decline our project?

Usually one of three reasons: no identifiable owner of the data or process, a
readiness score below 40/100, or a request to explore "what AI can do" without
a concrete workflow. We put declines in writing with the reason; many declined
prospects return after fixing the blocker and those engagements go well.
